# Full campaign and skill-tree simulation audit

**Historical pre-rebalance audit.** The resulting implementation and current results
are in [Gravity Well balance — 0.17.0](GRAVITY-WELL-BALANCE.md). Simulation output in
`build/progression-audit/` is regenerated by the current code; the figures below
describe the earlier balance.

October 30, 2026. Current eight-chapter / 201-node campaign, with the timing precision
fix described below. **The campaign completes, but the economy and encounter pacing
need another balancing pass.** No XP prices, material yields, upgrade effects, or
authored preparation times were changed during this audit.

## What was run

Six deterministic, in-memory campaigns from an empty career through all 40
destinations and ten first-clear bosses, ending with the neutron star. Every physics
step uses the production 60 Hz simulation. Each session saves and reloads the real
game/campaign codecs. No checkpoint grants, accelerated preparation, seeded XP,
materials, or ranks are used. These fixtures create no tablet or athlete-history files.

Free-play fixtures use up to 30 minutes per session and end at objective completion.
Balanced builds buy one affordable rank per expedition branch per session, plus
affordable foundation and specialist tools. Focused builds buy attraction, flow,
and erosion expedition branches plus the shared foundations; specialists are omitted.
These are reproducible shopping policies, not an assertion that players will choose
the same upgrades. Values below are measured results under those policies.

The physics-input/speed pairs are illustrative simulation controls, not watts,
fitness levels, or exercise prescriptions. All use the normal real-career rules in
memory, with synthetic activity confined to the audit process.

## How the tree scales

The catalog contains **201 definitions and 391 purchasable ranks**. Main-family
bonuses add within a stat family. Different families can compound; e.g. processing
and XP refinement multiply their effects on input conversion.

Measured standard balanced path, **after purchases at each destination**:

| Point | Owned ranks | Capacity | Attraction | Processing | XP refinement | Erosion | Material yield |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Empty career | 0 | 180 | 1.00× | 1.00× | 1.00× | 1.00× | 1.00× |
| Dwarf planet cleared | 50 | 600 | 2.04× | 2.12× | 2.40× | 1.32× | 1.40× |
| Ice giant cleared | 167 | 1,050 | 3.42× | 3.52× | 3.90× | 2.52× | 2.90× |
| Neutron star cleared | 181 | 1,080 | 3.50× | 3.60× | 4.00× | 2.52× | 2.90× |
| Entire tree purchased organically | 391 | 1,920 | 5.74× | 5.84× | 6.80× | 4.84× | 5.80× |

These are global factors before applicable target-specific specialist and fracture
bonuses. Capacity grows about 10.7×. The fully upgraded processing/refinement product
is about 39.7×, although actual XP also depends on stored matter versus inexhaustible
low-value dust. This is game economy scaling; input distance and workout targets
receive no multiplier.

**The plateau is the main finding:** ranks barely change from first starlight to
the finale. The standard build goes from 174 ranks after the brown dwarf to 181
after the neutron star, despite tens of additional hours and rapidly growing XP.
Source-specific boss materials, not XP, block most purchases. Ordinary preparation
does not substitute for those missing boss sources.

After the campaign, shopping is unrestricted and the fixture deliberately harvests
missing boss sources. All balanced trees reach 391/391 organically with **six repeat
bosses**, adding only **2.0–2.4 minutes**. The focused tree reaches its selected
175/175 ranks with five repeats and roughly 1.9 minutes. Total full-tree XP cost is
219,376; the standard build still holds about 576,592 XP after buying everything.

This demonstrates that the long plateau is avoidable through targeted harvesting,
not a permanent progression lock. It also exposes an abrupt catch-up: a small amount
of well-chosen harvesting releases most of the remaining tree at once. A naive
low-input strategy that repeatedly farms rare comet drops before strengthening reach
can make no progress; harvesting the deterministic boss materials first avoids that
trap. The audit's final strategy follows that productive route.

## Full trainer-led campaign

The sixth campaign uses the actual `TrainingCore.Runner` and `GameWorkoutAdapter`:
30-minute synthetic workout fixtures with a 3-minute warm-up, two 10-minute work
blocks, two 2-minute passive recoveries, and a 3-minute cool-down. Every workout runs
to completion regardless of an early campaign objective clear. New bosses wait for
an eligible work window. This fixture is a software test, not a proposed exercise plan.

It completes in **163 workouts / 81.5 session hours**, with 179 ranks and about
2.58 million unspent XP. Only WORK contributes the same 48-hour preparation budget.
Its reported 36.4 minutes from boss-destination start to first clear includes 30
minutes of warm-up waiting across ten bosses; it is not directly comparable to the
free-play encounter-time column.

Workout boundaries and objective selection create overhead beyond the work/recovery
ratio: an objective can finish midway through a session, but the next destination
cannot be selected until that session ends. Remaining work still earns ordinary
resources but cannot advance the next preparation objective. This is current product
behavior, not a runner failure. Consider an optional queued next preparation activity
so workout completion remains authoritative while campaign progress flows naturally.

## Bug reproduced and corrected

An exact 240-minute preparation budget accumulated at 60 Hz ended at
`14399.9999998746` seconds. The old completion tolerance was too small, leaving the
objective open until the next WORK block. In the guided fixture, this caused three
unnecessary additional 30-minute workouts across the final preparation objectives.

The completion tolerance is now one microsecond, far smaller than a single physics
frame. Thirty regression assertions cover every authored preparation duration,
checkpoint/reload continuity, no completion one frame early, and one-time completion.
The exact-frame regression failed before the fix and passes afterward. The guided
campaign fell from 166 to 163 sessions. The authored preparation budget is unchanged.

## Checks and remaining decisions

All six complete runs pass finite-XP, reservoir, population/particle-cap, checkpoint,
rank/material preservation, ten-first-clear, exact-input-distance, and XP accounting
checks. The existing sensor, game, migration, training and visual test suites also
pass, including 774 campaign/economy checks. No real career, athlete record, or
calibration was changed. This is model validation, not Android UI/performance QA or
an exercise-efficacy study. Overflow losses are not quantified by this harness.

Recommended next balance work:

1. Budget preparation and encounters together; avoid hours of preparation followed
   by a sub-minute featured encounter without intermediate meaningful events.
2. Smooth boss-material availability across each chapter, or clearly recommend
   repeat harvests before the next long preparation sequence.
3. Rebalance XP income, full-tree costs, and processing/refinement synergy together.
   Raising XP prices alone would leave the material plateau intact.
4. Test optional activity queuing during trainer-led sessions, keeping recovery,
   intensity targets, and workout end times unchanged.
5. Re-run these fixtures after tuning, including additional seeds and more player
   purchase strategies before treating the measured timings as typical player results.

## Reproducing the audit

Run `./simulate-progression.ps1` for all six paths, or supply e.g.
`-Scenario balanced-standard`. It compiles the pure Java models and uses no device.
The long simulation remains separate from `./test.ps1`.

`build/progression-audit/` contains per-destination metrics, per-boss timings,
individual purchase ledgers, completion summaries, post-campaign summaries, and
simulation-only save snapshots. Never import those snapshots into a real career.
Run `./test.ps1` for regressions and `./build.ps1` for the APK containing the precision fix.
